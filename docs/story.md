## Prologue · آغاز ("beginning") · 2017

> It started with one interview.
>
> In 2017 I watched an interview with the CEO of one of Silicon Valley's biggest tech
> companies, talking about where AI is taking us. I was hooked. Not by the hype, but by how
> clear the future looked when I gave myself space to think about it. I chose Computer
> Engineering in Tehran as my way in.

## Chapter I · ۱ · The monster

> Every idea grew into a monster.
>
> I never loved programming for its own sake. I loved building things, especially for health
> and medicine. But every idea grew into a monster in my head: too big to start, too big to
> finish. My first computer vision internship made it clear. Knowing things was never my
> problem. Finishing was.

## Chapter II · ۲ · The turning point

> Ship it small. *Then let it grow.*
>
> Moving to Austria changed that. Far from everything familiar, there was nowhere to hide from
> myself, so I stopped running from the fear of failing. Now I build the smallest working
> version first, ship it, then let it grow.
>
> I build with AI, not just on it. AI agents help me break a monster into small steps, draft
> and review. I make the calls, test everything, and understand every line I ship. This site
> is proof: built in small phases, every commit public on GitHub.

## Chapter III · ۳ · The proof

> Things that *work.*
>
> Real projects for real clients and real problems. Each one shows the problem, my approach,
> the result and a demo.

Featured projects follow (Phase 4).

## Chapter IV · ۴ · The why

> Why *medicine.*
>
> My mom lives with diabetes, and I've always been curious about how the body and the brain
> work. That's why I want to work where AI meets medicine: helping people live better with
> diseases like hers.

## Epilogue · فردا ("tomorrow") · Vienna

> Next stop: *Vienna.*
>
> Since I was a teenager, Vienna has been my picture of a modern city where you don't lose
> your soul: history, art and people in every street. That's where I want to build.
